Account Security

Close the gaps before someone else finds them. Most hacks start with one weak setting.

  • Security check of your accounts and Business Manager settings
  • Meta Verified and Business Manager verification for pages
  • Two-factor authentication and login alerts set up properly
  • Admin and page roles reviewed, old access removed
  • Admin roles, recovery email and phone confirmed

For: anyone whose page earns money or carries their name

Account Recovery

When you are locked out, disabled or hacked, I find out what happened and take you through the right recovery route.

  • Hacked accounts and changed email or phone
  • Disabled Facebook, Instagram and page unbans
  • Restricted or disabled LinkedIn accounts
  • Two-factor lockouts and lost Business Manager access
  • Securing the account once it is back

Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok

Online Reputation Management

Protect how people see you online and respond well when something goes wrong.

  • Monitoring mentions, comments and copycat profiles
  • Fake and impersonation accounts reported and removed through official channels
  • Response plan for complaints and bad reviews
  • Cleaning up and strengthening your official profiles
  • Content that rebuilds trust after a problem

For: brands, public figures, businesses after a scam or hack

How a recovery case works

  1. Tell me what happenedSend the profile link and what you see when you try to log in. Screenshots help.
  2. I identify the issueHacked, disabled, restricted or impersonated. Each needs a different route.
  3. I guide the official processWe go through the platform's own recovery and appeal steps, with the right evidence, and I follow up.
  4. Lock it downOnce you are back in, we set up two-factor, login alerts and clean admin access so it doesn't happen again.

An honest note. Platforms make the final decision on every account, so nobody can guarantee a recovery. I never ask for your password, never use hacking tools, and only work through official processes.
