01 About me

The person behind the handle

Two kinds of work, one goal: your presence online should be safe, steady and worth following.

For more than five years I've worked in social media management and account recovery, helping bring over 500 accounts back to their owners — disabled Facebook profiles, hacked Instagram business accounts, restricted LinkedIn accounts, lost Business Manager access and fake profiles using someone else's name.

I'm Creative Director at TechBite, where we recover accounts, verify business pages with Meta and train new team members. I also run DiGiHub, a page for social media management and online security.

Alongside that I design: logos, brand identities, social posts, carousels, reels and brand films for 300+ clients. Knowing how platforms work makes the design sharper — and knowing design makes the accounts I manage look professional.

I work in Bangla and English, remotely, with clients in Bangladesh, Pakistan, the UK and beyond.

02 Social media services

Manage. Secure. Recover. Repair.

Disabled account? Security issue? Lost access? A fake profile using your name? I identify the problem, guide the right process and help you get back on track.

Social Media Management

Day-to-day running of your pages so they grow with a plan instead of by chance.

  • Content plan and posting calendar
  • Captions & hashtags in Bangla and English
  • Reels, posts and stories designed to your brand
  • Page setup, bio and highlights clean-up
  • Monthly review of what worked and what to change
For: shops, service businesses, creators, organisations

Account Security

Close the gaps before someone else finds them. Most hacks start with one weak setting.

  • Security check of accounts & Business Manager
  • Meta Verified and Business verification for pages
  • Two-factor authentication & login alerts
  • Admin and page roles reviewed, old access removed
  • Recovery email and phone confirmed
For: anyone whose page earns money or carries their name

Account Recovery

Locked out, disabled or hacked — I find out what happened and take you through the right route.

  • Hacked accounts and changed email or phone
  • Disabled Facebook, Instagram and page unbans
  • Restricted or disabled LinkedIn accounts
  • Two-factor lockouts & lost Business Manager access
  • Securing the account once it's back
Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok

Online Reputation Management

Protect how people see you online and respond well when something goes wrong.

  • Monitoring mentions, comments & copycat profiles
  • Fake accounts reported and removed officially
  • Response plan for complaints and bad reviews
  • Cleaning up and strengthening official profiles
  • Content that rebuilds trust after a problem
For: brands, public figures, businesses after a scam or hack
