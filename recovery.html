FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you need login access to my account?+
Not up front. Triage starts from your IDs, screenshots, and case history. If access is ever needed, I'll explain exactly why and keep it to the minimum, with your permission.
Can you guarantee results?+
No honest specialist can guarantee a platform outcome. What I guarantee is a proper diagnosis, correct escalation, and a clear picture of what's possible before you commit.
How fast will this be fixed?+
Some cases move within days. Full stabilisation usually takes 2–8 weeks depending on the platform and how severe the restriction is. I set that expectation at intake.
Can you help if my account was hacked?+
Yes. Hacked assets, unauthorized admin changes, and compromised pages are a core part of the work — recovering access and locking things back down.
What platforms do you support?+
Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ad accounts, Business Manager and pages are the focus, with Google Ads account issues handled too.
What do you need from me to get started?+
Your Ad Account or Business Manager ID, screenshots of the error, your website/offer, and any recent changes to billing, domain, creatives, logins, or agency access.
